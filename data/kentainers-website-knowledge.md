## FAQ

Q: Where are Kentainers located?
A: Nairobi Gate Industrial Park, along Eastern Bypass, Nairobi, Kenya. The official site also lists factories in Mombasa and Kisumu.

Q: Are the tanks double layered?
A: Kentainers states that it has both double-layered and single-layered tanks; its big tanks are mostly single layered.

Q: What are the opening hours?
A: Monday-Friday 8:30 am-5:30 pm; Saturday 8:30 am-1:30 pm; closed Sundays and public holidays. Website purchases can be made anytime.

Q: Do Kentainers have stockists?
A: Kentainers states that it has stockists countrywide, but customers should check with the sales office to confirm area coverage.

Q: What products does Kentainers sell besides tanks?
A: Water storage and supply products, low-cost sanitation products, material-handling products, and agricultural products.

Q: Does Kentainers offer installation?
A: Yes, installation is offered at a cost and can be discussed with the sales team.

Q: Can customers pay in installments?
A: Yes, through Kentainers' bank and MFI partners rather than directly through Kentainers.

Q: What is the product lifespan?
A: The FAQ states an expected lifespan of 30 years. Product-specific documentation should be consulted for exact product guidance.

Q: What are delivery timelines?
A: Delivery varies by location and route load and may take 3-7 working days.

Q: What payment methods are accepted?
A: The FAQ states that Kentainers accepts modern payment methods including mobile money and card payments.

Q: What other services are provided?
A: Free pre- and post-sale services including technical and operational guidance.

Q: Which countries does Kentainers operate in?
A: Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda and Zambia.

## Product knowledge

### Kentank
Kentank is an above-ground water storage tank. Official product documentation describes food-grade polyethylene construction, corrugated design, one-piece moulding without joints, and applications including indoor/outdoor water storage, food-grade chemical storage, water harvesting, water treatment plants, agriculture, and domestic/commercial uses. The product documentation states a 30-year expected outdoor lifespan.

### Bunkatank
Bunkatank is an underground water storage tank made from food-grade polyethylene. Documented capacities include BKT 200 = 2,000 L, BKT 250 = 2,500 L, BKT 300 = 3,000 L, and BKT 600 = 6,000 L. Applications include water storage, water harvesting, water treatment plants, agriculture, and domestic/commercial uses.

### KPOT
KPOT is a water storage pot. Documented variants include KPOT 3 = 30 L and KPOT 6 = 60 L, with versions with tap and accessories. Applications include indoor/outdoor water storage, chemical storage, agriculture, and hand-wash systems. It is described as food-grade virgin material with in-built handles.

### Loftank
Loftank is a rectangular water storage tank. Documented capacities include LR 23 = 230 L, LR 46 = 460 L, and LR 100 = 920 L. Applications include indoor/outdoor water storage, water harvesting, water treatment plants, agriculture, apartments and commercial uses.

### Nestank
Nestank is a nestable water storage tank. Documented variants include NST 50 = 500 L and NST 100 = 1,000 L. Applications include indoor/outdoor water storage, food-grade chemical storage, water harvesting, water treatment plants, agriculture, and domestic/commercial uses.

### Eco Drum
Eco Drum is a single-layered polyethylene water drum. Documented variants include DR 10 = 100 L and DR 20 = 200 L. Features include in-built handles and corrugation for strength. Applications include indoor/outdoor use, agriculture, water storage, homes, schools and hotels.

### Fish Tub
The official website lists Fish Tub in Agriculture & Biogas, with 600 L and 800 L size options. The product page describes door-to-door delivery and countrywide distribution through the dealer/stockist network.

### Grain Silo
The official website lists Grain Silo in Agriculture & Biogas. The product page describes a 500 L grain silo and provides shipping zones and delivery information.

### Kenpallet 130
Kenpallet 130 is a material-handling pallet. The official product page states a pallet size of 130 cm x 110 cm x 15 cm, static weight of 3 tonnes, dynamic weight of 2 tonnes, four-way entry, and uses including stacking/racking, cold rooms, wet areas, refrigerated trucks and air circulation.
