KENTANK ABOVE-GROUND WATER STORAGE (CATEGORY W1)
Kentank is an above-ground water storage tank made from food-grade polyethylene. The official Kentainers specification states an expected outdoor lifespan of 30 years, a corrugated design, one-piece moulding with no joints, and applications including outdoor and indoor water storage, food-grade chemical storage, water harvesting, water treatment plants, agriculture, and domestic/commercial uses.

PEDAL HAND WASH (CATEGORY S5)
Pedal Hand Wash is made from food-grade polyethylene. Features include no hand contact, hygiene, and ease of operation. Available capacities are 30 L, 60 L, 100 L and 200 L. It is supplied with a tap, basin, pipe and 20 L collection bucket. Stands are available in mild steel and stainless steel. Listed applications include homes, SMEs, schools, churches/mosques, and IDP/refugee camps.

KPOT (WATER STORAGE POT, CATEGORY W4)
Kpot is a small portable water-storage product available in 30 L and 60 L sizes. It is made from food-grade virgin polyethylene and has in-built handles. Listed applications include indoor/outdoor water storage, chemical storage, agriculture and hand-wash systems.

DRUM (CATEGORY W6)
Kentainers drums are double-layered and made from food-grade polyethylene. DRUM 10 is 100 L with height 62 cm and diameter 48 cm. DRUM 20 is 200 L with height 77 cm and diameter 58 cm. Listed applications include indoor/outdoor water storage, chemical storage, agriculture and hand-wash systems.

AGRICULTURE AND SCHOOL USE
Kentank is explicitly listed for agriculture and domestic/commercial uses. Kpot and Drum are also listed for agriculture. Kentainers' current shop includes agriculture/biogas products, sanitation/waste products, material-handling products and water-storage products. Pedal Hand Wash is explicitly listed for schools. Ekoslab UD is listed by Kentainers for schools, clinics, IDP/refugee settings, army camps, institutions, farms, homes and construction sites.

6,000 LITRE CLARIFICATION
A request for 6,000 L should clarify application because Kentainers has more than one 6,000 L solution. CCV 600 is a 6,000 L above-ground water storage tank. BKT 600 is a 6,000 L underground water storage tank. A separate official zonal price list also lists SPT 600 as a 6,000 L septic tank. Therefore KPIA should ask whether the customer means above-ground water storage, underground water storage, or sanitation/septic use.

PRICE AND COMMERCIAL INFORMATION
Kentainers publishes zonal price lists. The 15 April 2026 zonal Kentank price list shows CCV 600 at different VAT-inclusive prices by delivery zone, so price depends on delivery zone and price-list date. Do not present an old price as a current quotation. Always confirm the latest price with Kentainers before quoting.

WARRANTY VS EXPECTED LIFESPAN
The official Kentainers FAQ states that the expected lifespan of products is 30 years and refers customers to installation guidelines. This is not the same as a stated warranty period. KPIA must not claim that 30 years is a warranty period unless Kentainers confirms that separately.

CUSTOMER ENQUIRY INFORMATION
Before a product recommendation, KPIA should capture customer name, email and phone number, then clarify the product requirement. For tanks, useful requirements include capacity, intended application, installation context and any relevant space/location constraints. If the requirement remains ambiguous, KPIA should ask a concise clarification question rather than inventing a recommendation.
