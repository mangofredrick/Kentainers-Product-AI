# Kentainers Technical & Product Knowledge Library

**Library purpose:** Source-grounded knowledge for the Kentainers Product Chatbot. This file complements the structured product catalogue, pricing data and FAQ data. It is intended to be indexed by the RAG retriever and expanded as additional official Kentainers PDFs and website pages are added.

**Source policy:** Use official Kentainers website pages and Kentainers/Aquasantec product documents. Do not invent specifications, engineering values, stock, delivery dates, prices or certifications. Commercial prices must be treated as date- and location-sensitive.

## 7. PEDAL HAND WASH

Pedal Hand Wash is made from food-grade polyethylene. Listed features include no hand contact, hygiene, and ease of operation. Capacities are 30 L, 60 L, 100 L and 200 L. The product is supplied with a tap, basin, pipe and 20 L collection bucket. Mild-steel and stainless-steel stands are available. Listed applications include homes, SMEs, schools, churches/mosques and IDP/refugee camps.

## 8. SANITATION PRODUCTS

Kentainers' official product material includes sanitation solutions such as slabs and WonderLoo. The official slabs document describes simple, low-cost ecological sanitation, quick installation, privacy/dignity and separation of urine and faeces. Listed applications include schools, clinics, IDP/refugee and army camps, institutions, farms, homes and construction sites.

## 12. INSTALLATION — KENTANK

Official Kentank installation instructions state that the tank should be placed on a fully supported flat base with no gaps, and the base should be bigger than the tank diameter. Inlet and outlet holes should be drilled at specified locations using a drill machine. An overflow should be provided and water should be drained away from the base. Plastic fittings and flexible pipes are recommended when interconnecting two tanks, and a rubber washer should be used between the tank and back nut.

The installation instructions specifically warn against drilling holes with a hot metal rod or knife and against leaving stones or sharp objects on the tank base. The chatbot should present these as manufacturer installation guidance rather than independent engineering advice.

## 13. PRICING KNOWLEDGE

Kentainers publishes zonal price lists. The official 2026 price list uses delivery-distance zones and states that prices include VAT. The list also states that prices may change without notice and that zones 7 and 8 may vary according to point of delivery.

Price answers must therefore include:
1. product code/name;
2. capacity/variant where applicable;
3. delivery zone/location;
4. price-list effective date;
5. whether VAT is included where the source states it;
6. a confirmation note that the latest commercial quotation should be verified before ordering.

Never combine a historical price with a current-date statement. If the customer asks for a price without a delivery location where pricing is zonal, ask for the location.
