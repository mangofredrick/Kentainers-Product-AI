## Delivery and distribution

Official website product pages describe a door-to-door delivery policy across the country. The site says prices may be inclusive of delivery depending on product/zone and that a delivery charge may apply outside the stated free zone. Customers are directed to contact Kentainers to verify a stockist or delivery arrangement for their location.

The zonal 2026 Kentank price list defines delivery-distance zones and states that prices are for delivery to towns listed in each zone. It also states that zones 7 and 8 may vary according to point of delivery and that prices may change without notice.

## Customer service / FAQ

The official FAQ states:
- Nairobi location: Nairobi Gate Industrial Park, along Eastern Bypass, Nairobi.
- Opening hours: Monday-Friday 8:30 am to 5:30 pm; Saturday 8:30 am to 1:30 pm; closed Sundays and public holidays.
- Kentainers has both single-layered and double-layered tanks; the FAQ says large tanks are mostly single layered.
- Stockists are available countrywide, with area coverage to be confirmed with the sales office.
- Installation services are available at a cost.
- The FAQ discusses installment purchasing through bank/MFI partners rather than treating it as a standard direct Kentainers payment plan.
- The FAQ describes an expected product lifespan of 30 years. This must not be presented as a warranty period unless a separate warranty source explicitly says so.
- Delivery timelines can vary; the FAQ gives 3–7 working days as a typical range.
- The website states that modern payment methods including mobile money and card payments are accepted.

## Current price-answer rules

When a customer asks "How much?":
1. Identify the exact product and variant.
2. Identify capacity where applicable.
3. Identify delivery location because Kentainers pricing may be zonal.
4. Prefer the newest official price source available.
5. State the price-list effective date.
6. State VAT treatment where the source specifies it.
7. Warn that published prices may change without notice.
8. If the website gives only "Request For Quote", do not invent a price.

## Important product disambiguation

A question such as "How much is a 6,000 litre Kentank?" is ambiguous. Official Kentainers material includes:
- CCV 600 — 6,000 L above-ground water storage
- BKT 600 — 6,000 L underground water storage
- SPT 600 — 6,000 L septic tank

Ask the intended application before quoting a product or price when the product identity is ambiguous.
